            How to read the labels

            Kind of source. Official: agency, regulator or court record. Secondary: news or reference reporting. Community: residents' or groups' accounts. Self-reported: the company's own statements. Derived: estimated or modeled for this map.

            Kind of claim. Alleged / Filed · allegation: stated in a complaint or filing, not proven. Regulator-stated: stated by the agency. Permitted / Approved: an issued permit or vote. Ruled: a court ruling. Reported: published account. Estimated: computed or modeled. Excerpt: checked only from a search excerpt; read the source before quoting.

            Compiled from public records by The Counter Map. Verify each source before relying on it in a filing.
